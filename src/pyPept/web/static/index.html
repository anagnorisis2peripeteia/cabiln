Working with CABILN

Start guided tutorial ↗ Build a short peptide in a separate practice tab. Your current work stays here.

Type a sequence, or choose SMILES, BILN or HELM and convert it. Open Build, select a residue in the drawing or its tile, then choose Use in the library and choose free numbered sites. Click or tap an atom label or bond to select; drag to pan. Preview shows the proposed molecule; Connect applies it. Nested brackets keep their own attachment parent. Undo and Redo restore sequence edits.

To replace a residue, choose Swap monomer in Build. The library filters replacements against every existing connection. Review the numbered site mapping and preview the full product, then Apply swap. Site numbers can differ; the preview shows any reformatted notation. Undo restores the original.

Keep layout preserves your grouping. Canonical exports order the resolved monomer graph under a recorded convention and library binding. Imported structures can include inferred stereochemistry or preserved synthetic regions; open Import quality for details. Valid synthetic sites remain editable.

Save project downloads your source, notation drafts, original reference and conversion details. Opening checks the definitions and reaction rules before loading. PNG and MOL export the current drawing and molecule.

Drawing controls work with mouse, touch and keyboard. Focus a drawing and use arrow keys to pan, + or − to zoom, and 0 or Home to reset. Escape closes the active panel and returns focus to its button.

Your inputs and uploaded structures are sent to the server hosting this page for chemistry processing. Browser drafts stay in this browser's local storage; project files download to your device. Clearing a draft removes that browser copy only. Avoid putting private structures in public feedback.

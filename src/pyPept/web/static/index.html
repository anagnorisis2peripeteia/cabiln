Working with CABILN

Type a sequence, or choose SMILES, BILN or HELM and convert it. Open Build, select a residue, then choose Use in the library and connect free numbered sites. Nested brackets keep their own attachment parent. Undo and Redo restore sequence edits.

Keep layout preserves your grouping. Canonical exports order the resolved monomer graph under a recorded convention and library binding. Imported structures can include inferred stereochemistry or preserved synthetic regions; open Import quality for details. Valid synthetic sites remain editable.

Save project downloads your source, notation drafts, original reference and conversion details. Opening checks the definitions and reaction rules before loading. PNG and MOL export the current drawing and molecule.

Your inputs and uploaded structures are sent to the server hosting this page for chemistry processing. Browser drafts stay in this browser's local storage; project files download to your device. Clearing a draft removes that browser copy only. Avoid putting private structures in public feedback.
